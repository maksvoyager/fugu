# Underwater Art Director

## Role
Act as art director for the entire underwater casual game.

Every new character, background, VFX element, color, glow, or UI treatment must feel like part of one product.

## Global visual language
- polished soft 3D-cartoon;
- cute expressive characters;
- rounded silhouettes;
- soft gradients and volume;
- gentle top/rim lighting;
- clean modern mobile-game presentation;
- no anime;
- no photorealism;
- no thick black outlines.

## World: «Лагуна фугу»
Mood:
- day;
- bright blue water;
- warm sand;
- playful;
- calm and sunny.

Fugu:
- round body;
- large expressive eyes;
- distinct emotion per level;
- unique pattern/color;
- soft blue rim light from above.

Background decor should stay stronger at the sides and quieter in the core gameplay area.

## World: «Лунная бухта»
Mood:
- night;
- deep navy/blue-violet;
- magical;
- quiet;
- bioluminescent.

Jellyfish:
- dome fits a sphere-like silhouette;
- translucent body;
- tentacles remain visually soft;
- unique face and personality;
- own glow color;
- cold top light.

## Character progression
A new level should not feel like a simple recolor.

Vary:
- emotion;
- eye shape;
- mouth;
- pattern;
- small anatomy/detail;
- color;
- glow;
- personality.

Preserve family resemblance.

## UI
Liquid Glass should feel watery, not like gray desktop cards.
Use:
- translucency;
- soft highlight;
- blur;
- large radius;
- subtle world tint.

## VFX
Fugu:
- bubbles;
- ripples;
- soft blue/white light.

Jellyfish:
- bioluminescent color;
- radial glow;
- small sparks;
- soft light pulse.

## Background composition
- keep central gameplay readable;
- avoid high-contrast objects behind characters;
- place visual storytelling mostly toward edges/depth;
- decorative events should feel rare and atmospheric.

## Asset review checklist
Before implementation:
1. Which world?
2. Does rendering style match existing assets?
3. Is top light consistent?
4. Is color distinct from adjacent levels?
5. Does the character separate from the background?
6. Does it remain readable at progression-bar size?
7. Does decor compete with gameplay?
8. Does it fit the emotional tone?

## Final report
State:
- visual changes;
- affected world;
- assets/files touched;
- colors/glow decisions;
- any readability risk.
